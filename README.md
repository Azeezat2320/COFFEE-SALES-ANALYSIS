# COFFEE-SALES-ANALYSIS
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an analytical report on coffee sales data collected from a vending machine between March 2024 and March 2025.

The objective of this analysis was to uncover purchasing behavior patterns, identify seasonal sales trends, understand customer preferences, and evaluate payment method usage to support operational and business decision-making.

🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering the following key questions:

What are the purchasing patterns based on time of day?

How do sales vary across quarters?

What are the most preferred coffee types?

What payment methods do customers prefer?

📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains transactional records of coffee sales, including:

Date (datetime)

Coffee type

Time grouping (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)

Price range

Payment method (Card, Cash)

Sales amount

Time period covered: March 2024 – March 2025

📈 Key Insights
1️⃣ Purchasing Pattern by Time of Day

Sales are highest during the Afternoon and Evening periods.

Morning sales are comparatively lower.

Business Implication:

Increase staffing during Afternoon and Evening periods.

Ensure vending machines are fully stocked and operational before peak hours.

2️⃣ Quarterly Sales Trend

Higher sales were recorded in Quarter 1 (Q1) and Quarter 4 (Q4).

Sales appear to increase during colder months (winter season).

Business Implication:

Increase inventory and operational readiness during winter.

Allocate more resources during Q1 and Q4.

3️⃣ Customer Coffee Preferences

Certain coffee types consistently outperform others (as seen in the quarterly breakdown).

Seasonal demand influences specific product performance.

Business Implication:

Prioritize stocking of top-performing coffee types during peak quarters.

Use quarterly trends for demand forecasting.

4️⃣ Preferred Payment Method

Card payments significantly exceed cash payments.

Business Implication:

Provide additional POS/card payment terminals.

Reduce reliance on cash handling.

Optimize checkout efficiency.
